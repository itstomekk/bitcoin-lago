Lake Atitlán, and the Bitcoin community that lives here

Three volcanoes, Maya villages along the shore, and a strong group of people who pay, teach and welcome with Bitcoin.

A lake ringed by three volcanoes

Lake Atitlán fills a volcanic basin in the highlands of Sololá, Guatemala. The volcanoes Atitlán, Tolimán and San Pedro rise along its south shore.

Maya villages sit along the water. Kaqchikel and Tz’utujil are the main Maya languages spoken around the lake, and every village has its own character.

Panajachel is the busiest gateway. From its docks, public boats cross to San Marcos La Laguna, San Pedro La Laguna, Santiago Atitlán and other villages.

    Bitcoin in schools

    Since 2023 we teach Bitcoin courses with a diploma, from primary schools to universities. Students make their first Lightning payments in class.

    So far: Centro Educativo Josué, LTK School, Colegio Ebenezer, AMA School, INEDSC in Santa Catarina Palopó, Ciencias Comerciales in Sololá (200 students per course) and UVG Altiplano.

    What Atitlán Bitcoin Tours offers

    • Hikes and volcanoes. Easy, medium and hard hikes on the mountains and volcanoes around the lake, with viewpoints and the largest caves on the lake. Source
    • Volcán de Fuego. A personal plan to see an active volcano up close. Source
    • ATVs and paragliding. Ride around the lake on ATVs, or fly over it by paraglider. Source
    • Coffee, cacao and honey. Coffee tours with Bit Mayan Coffee, how cacao is grown and roasted, and a honey tour. Source
    • Weaving and natural dyes. See how Maya weavers spin cotton yarn and dye it with fruits, seeds and plants, and try the loom yourself. Source
    • The Palopó towns. San Antonio Palopó for ceramics, hot springs and a viewpoint, and colourful Santa Catarina Palopó. Source
    • Santa Cruz, Tzununá and Jaibalito. Villages by boat: Santa Cruz La Laguna seven minutes from Panajachel, Tzununá with its waterfalls, and Jaibalito, reached only by water. Source
    • Chichicastenango market. One of the biggest markets in Guatemala, where Maya ceremonies are still held. Source

    Visiting the lake

    • Lake days by boat: the villages, sunrise and sunset rides, the Bitcoin boat
    • Hikes and adventure: Rostro Maya at sunrise, San Pedro volcano, viewpoints, kayaks, paragliding, ATVs
    • Culture and craft: weaving and natural dyes, chocolate, honey, coffee, cooking with a Maya family, the Sololá and Chichicastenango markets
    • Getting here and around: shuttles from the airport or Antigua, private boats, tuk tuks, scooters and bikes

    Working and living here

    • Stays that accept bitcoin: hotels, apartments and villas, for a week or a season
    • A soft landing: SIM card, internet good enough for calls, coworking, cash and banks, the right town for you
    • Moving here: introductions to long rentals, land, builders and local lawyers for residency
    • Spanish classes and homestays with local families

    Groups and teams

    Our story

    Bitcoin Lago began in Panajachel in 2021. It was founded by Patrick Melder with friends and supporters, out of years of volunteering at Centro Educativo Josué. Eliazar, our local leader, started by visiting businesses one by one.

    One of my main goals with Bitcoin Lago is simply to teach about Bitcoin to all the communities around Lake Atitlán, from 10-year-old children to 100-year-old adults. One of my greatest dreams is for every town around the lake to learn about Bitcoin and use it, and one day to open a Bitcoin center here.

    Eliazar, Bitcoin Lago

    Year by year

    1. 2021

      Bitcoin Lago starts in Panajachel. Eliazar visits businesses one by one, and the first merchants accept bitcoin.

    2. 2022

      Kaboom: used cooking oil runs a generator that mines bitcoin. The lake team travels to Adopting Bitcoin in El Salvador. Source: 1 2

    3. 2023

      Bitcoin enters the classroom: diploma courses at Centro Educativo Josué and a first course for teachers. The Bitcoin Beach team visits and rides the Bitcoin boat. Tuk tuks start taking bitcoin. Source: 1 2 3

    4. 2024

      Courses at LTK School, Colegio Ebenezer, AMA School and INEDSC in Santa Catarina Palopó. Students open their first Fedi community. Wallets and books arrive from the Swiss Bitcoin community. Talks at UVG in Sololá. Source: 1 2 3

    5. 2025

      A Bitcoin shelf opens at the Panajachel public library. Our first university course, at UVG Altiplano. 200 students at Ciencias Comerciales in Sololá. Classes in the local Maya language in San Antonio Palopó, and bitcoin reaches Santiago Atitlán. Source: 1 2 3 4

    6. 2026

      The first graduation of the year is recorded on the Bitcoin blockchain. 200 more students in Sololá, a Lightning workshop for a women's group, a container hotel and Chui Juyub coffee join, and Atitlán Bitcoin Tours launches. Source: 1 2 3 4

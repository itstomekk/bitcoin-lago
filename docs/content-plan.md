# Lago Bitcoin: content plan (2026-10-21)

## Services research: what visitors, nomads and relocators look for around Panajachel

Sources read 2026-10-21: atastefortravel.ca (21 things to do in Panajachel), tours-atitlan.com, atitlanexperience.com,
GetYourGuide and Tripadvisor Panajachel listings, atitlanvida.com (remote work, expat life, where to stay),
livinginguatemala.com (Panajachel, cost of living, coworking), theleannomad.com, nomad.watch, sababaresort.com,
retreat venues (Villa Sumaya, Eagle's Nest, Alma Atitlán, Villas del Carmen, Casa Paloma, Sacred Garden).

Most booked tourist services
- Boat day to the villages (San Juan: chocolate, honey, textiles; Santiago; San Pedro), sunset and sunrise boats.
- Rostro Maya (Indian Nose) sunrise hike, Volcán San Pedro, kayak + hike, paragliding, ATVs to Santa Catarina, ziplines.
- Chichicastenango and Sololá markets, cooking class with a Maya family, coffee and chocolate tours, weaving.
- Shuttles from Guatemala City airport and Antigua, private boats, tuk tuks, scooter and paddle board rental (Tekoa, Panajachel, from Eliazar's posts).

Digital nomads
- Internet you can take calls on (Panajachel has fiber; villages rely on 4G and Starlink), coworking, a Tigo SIM, power backup.
- First week in a flexible stay, then a long rental found by word of mouth (Facebook groups, asking locally).
- Town choice: Panajachel for infrastructure, San Pedro for budget and social, San Marcos for wellness, Santa Cruz and Jaibalito for quiet.

Relocators
- 90-day CA-4 tourist limit; a remote worker residency category exists since October 2025 (IGM-016/017-2025). Needs a local lawyer.
- Long rentals, land (200 m lakeshore restriction on foreign ownership), builders, healthcare (Sololá hospital, Hospitalito in Santiago).

Teams and groups
- Retreat venues hold 9 to 45 people; groups want one coordinator for venue, transport, food and activities.

How this became the site
- "Plan your stay" lists four groups (Visiting, Working and living, Groups and teams, Support the work), no prices.
- One form opens WhatsApp with the request written. Lago Bitcoin arranges it with local providers, payment in bitcoin,
  and onboards each provider to accept bitcoin.

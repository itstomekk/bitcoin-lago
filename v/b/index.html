Three volcanoes, Maya villages along the shore, and a strong group of people who pay, teach and welcome with Bitcoin.

A lake ringed by three volcanoes

Lake Atitlán fills a volcanic basin in the highlands of Sololá, Guatemala. The volcanoes Atitlán, Tolimán and San Pedro rise along its south shore.

Maya villages sit along the water. Kaqchikel and Tz’utujil are the main Maya languages spoken around the lake, and every village has its own character.

Panajachel is the busiest gateway. From its docks, public boats cross to San Marcos La Laguna, San Pedro La Laguna, Santiago Atitlán and other villages.

    Where the merchant network started

    The first Bitcoin merchant network around the lake was started by Bitcoin Lake (Lago Bitcoin), founded by Patrick Melder in Panajachel in 2021 and rooted in years of volunteering at Centro Educativo Josué. We are a separate, independent community and this site is not run by Bitcoin Lake.
